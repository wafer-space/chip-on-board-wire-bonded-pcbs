2Hole-to-copper clearance Decided

Hold every hole wall 0.23 mm from other copper. Two settings clear about a third of the spots; the rest is moving copper.

Today Rev 0 flagged two spots: a trace 0.200 mm from via /pad_54, and PWR_AUX_1 on B.Cu 0.201 mm from U102 pad 77 (a die-pad hole). About 150 other spots on the 1x1 are just as close. Its report gives no counts, so the plan clears them all in one pass.

To scale. The same 0.23 mm applies to tracks, teardrops and the GND pour: measured from the hole wall, not the pad edge.

Rev 0's page is nextpcb.com/rev0-pcba, read on 2026-11-09. 0.23 mm is NextPCB's published 0.229 mm plated-hole limit, and also their 0.178 mm via limit plus room for the drill to grow 0.1 mm.

The checks

Before each upload: KiCad's own DRC with Board Setup's minimum hole clearance at 0.23 mm. Every remaining spot shows as a hole clearance error.

The gate: Rev 0's DFM report on the uploaded single-board zip.

check_board.py and the Standard PCB limits (no longer the gate)

check_board.py checks NextPCB's Standard PCB limits from nextpcb.kicad_dru. Some are stricter than what Rev 0 accepted on the 1x1, so it will keep printing FAIL, for example on the 0.075 mm via ring and the 0.15 mm pad-to-pad rule. It's still the only tool here that measures copper against V-cut lines, which matters only if we panelize again.
