3The limits and the check

One command decides whether a board passes, using NextPCB's published Standard PCB limits.

 
python planning/nextpcb_redesign/check/check_board.py --designs
python planning/nextpcb_redesign/check/check_board.py --panels
python planning/nextpcb_redesign/check/check_board.py run-2/1x1-cob/1x1-mezzanine.kicad_pcb

It works on a temporary copy and checks saved copper, so refill zones and save in KiCad first. On a panel it also measures copper against the V-cut lines, which KiCad's own DRC can't see because they sit on User.1. The rules are in nextpcb.kicad_dru. Don't copy that file next to a real board: its base clearance rule makes zone refills pour too close.

Message for NextPCB

Ready to send once you've confirmed the order details in Q3.

We're preparing a 4-layer, 0.8 mm V-cut panel of wire-bonded chip-on-board PCBs, with ENEPIG finish and 1 oz outer copper. Could you confirm the following before we finish the design?

1. Our bond fingers sit together in one open solder mask window, with no mask between them. Three designs have 0.2 mm fingers at 0.3 mm pitch, a 0.10 mm gap. Your SMD pad-to-pad limit is 0.15 mm, and your general spacing limit is 3 mil. Is the 0.10 mm gap acceptable here?
2. We have 0.3 mm plated holes in a GND die pad, with 0.5 or 0.6 mm pads. Will you treat these as vias rather than component PTH, given the 0.2 mm PTH annular ring on your capabilities page?
3. We plan vias with a 0.45 mm pad and 0.25 mm finished hole. Your page says holes are enlarged 0.125 mm in production. Is the annular ring acceptable after that?
4. ENEPIG is on the Advanced PCB quote. Do the Standard PCB limits apply, or does Advanced allow anything tighter? What Ni, Pd and Au thicknesses do you offer, and are they suitable for wire bonding?
5. Can you build our custom stackup (0.196 mm prepreg, 0.23 mm core, 0.196 mm prepreg, 1 oz inner copper) to 0.8 mm finished?
6. V-cut lines are in our outline Gerber along with the board outline, labelled V-CUT. Is that acceptable, or do you want them in a separate file?
