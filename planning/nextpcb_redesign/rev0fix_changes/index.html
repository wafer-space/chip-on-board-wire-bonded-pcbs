Tool diff

Changes to the repo tools since the 1x1 commit. Most were needed for the 0p5x1, which is much denser around U102 than the other boards. The 0p5x0p5 and 1x0p5 were done first, with the earlier tools.

  • solve.py sees more obstacles. It now sees copper graphics, such as the logo on the 1x0p5 that a via had been moved into, and pad-specific clearances (U102's die-pad holes need 0.18 mm). It also catches two tracks that would cross after a move, not only ends that come too close.
  • solve.py keeps the pour connected. A via may not move closer to a pad that only the pour feeds (17 such pads on the 0p5x1). An item near the board edge may move toward it only while the pour strip between them stays 0.25 mm wide. Without these, the 0p5x1's bottom GND strip was cut and U101's MP pads were stranded.
  • New rev0fix.py options. --ignore-fp REF (U102 exemption), --ignore-cu, --edge (vias and tracks to 0.45 mm, pour kept at 0.5 mm), --near, --reach. When DRC is already clean, --export runs no rounds, so hand fixes are kept.
  • New hand-fix scripts. movefp.py moves a footprint and drags its track ends (C108). nudge.py moves one via or corner and drags its track ends (the two 0p5x1 hand fixes).
  • pcbdiff/extract.py now records each pad's own clearance and the copper graphics, for the solver and the diff.
